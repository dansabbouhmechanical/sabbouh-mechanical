Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. If a walk-in, rooftop unit, or ice machine goes down, call us any time at (905) 252-6106. We answer 24/7 and our trucks stay stocked so we can diagnose and repair on the first visit.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Newmarket and serve the Greater Toronto Area — including Toronto, Markham, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Aurora, Bradford, Stouffville, Whitby, Oshawa, Brampton, and Barrie. See the full list on our About page.

Do you only work on Hoshizaki ice machines?

No. We're Hoshizaki-certified, and we also service every other major commercial ice machine brand, including Scotsman and Snooker.

Do you do residential work too?

Yes. We install, maintain, and repair furnaces, air conditioners, and water tanks, and handle gas appliance hookups for stoves and BBQ lines. See residential services.

What sizes of refrigeration trailers do you rent?

We rent 8'×8' and 10'×8' mobile trailers. Both work as fully operational walk-in coolers or freezers, and delivery, hookup, and pickup are included. See trailer rentals.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Sabbouh Mechanical is TSSA-licensed for gas, refrigeration, and HVAC work, and fully insured for commercial and residential jobs.

How do I get a quote?

Call (905) 252-6106 or online. We'll get back to you the same day with honest pricing and no obligation.
