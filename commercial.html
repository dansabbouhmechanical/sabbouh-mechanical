Commercial refrigeration

Walk-ins and kitchen refrigeration in Newmarket and the GTA

Walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-ins, prep tables, and display cases are the core of the commercial work. Typical service includes compressor diagnostics and replacement, refrigerant leak detection and recharge, and defrost and temperature-control calibration. The same visit can cover the rooftop unit or the kitchen hood if that is what is actually down.
