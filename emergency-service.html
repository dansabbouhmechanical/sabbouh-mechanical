When equipment is down

Call first. We answer the phone.

Sabbouh Mechanical is based in Newmarket. The company has been family-owned since 1995 and offers 24-hour emergency service for commercial refrigeration, heating, and air conditioning. Downtime is not an option for the kitchens that rely on us, so the emergency line reaches a real person.

When you call, tell us the equipment, what it is doing, and the city. A walk-in that will not hold temperature, an ice machine that stopped producing, a rooftop unit down before service, or a furnace out at home are all calls we take. Office hours on the contact page are Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. The phone itself is answered 24/7.

We arrive equipped to diagnose the issue and restore the equipment. If you still need cold storage while a walk-in is being repaired or replaced, we rent 8′×8′ and 10′×8′ refrigeration trailers that run as coolers or freezers. Delivery, hookup, and pickup are included.
