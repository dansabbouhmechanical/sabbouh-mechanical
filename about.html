30 Years of Trusted Service

A family-owned commercial HVAC and refrigeration company based in Newmarket. Built on word of mouth across York Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

Sabbouh Mechanical is a family-owned and operated company based in Newmarket, with more than 30 years of experience delivering expert commercial refrigeration, heating, and air conditioning services. We take great pride in our workmanship and are committed to providing prompt, reliable solutions our clients can count on.

Serving commercial clients throughout York Region and the Greater Toronto Area — including Aurora, Richmond Hill, Markham, Vaughan, and Toronto — our business continues to grow through word of mouth. This reputation reflects the integrity, dedication, and craftsmanship we bring to every job. The cities are listed on the service area page.

We understand that downtime is not an option for the businesses that rely on us. That's why we offer 24-hour emergency service, arriving fully equipped to accurately diagnose the issue and restore your equipment to full operation with minimal disruption. That includes ice machines and, when you need cold storage in the meantime, refrigeration trailer rentals.

In addition to our commercial expertise, we also provide professional installation, maintenance, and repair services for residential furnaces and air conditioning systems — bringing the same standard of quality and care to every home we serve. Call (905) 252-6106 or request a quote.
